## The change

STD-ARCH-001 says: *Prefer extending an existing service behind a stable interface over introducing a new deployable component.* STD-SEC-002 says: *Send AI services only the fields the use case needs. Attachments and personal data must not be sent.* A label lists the claims a right answer may state, and one of them is enough. One was added to each, in [`eval_cases.jsonl`](../eval_cases.jsonl):

| Case | `states` before | Added |
| --- | --- | --- |
| E01 | `inside* existing`, `in the existing* api`, `extend* the existing`, `keep* it in the existing` | `prefer* the existing* api` |
| E04 | `only* field*`, `minimal* payload`, `minimum* field*`, `minimal* field*`, `minimum* payload`, `minimi* set of field*`, `allowlist* field*` | `allowlist* necessar* field*` |

A claim's words must come in order, in one clause, with at most three other words between one and the next (`CLAIM_GAP` in [`checks.py`](../function/advisor_core/checks.py)). With *necessary* between them, up to seven words can separate *allowlist* and *fields*. Read by today's `checks.py` with the new labels:

### The misread that stays

E04, `gpt-6-luna`, agents version 1, the fresh setup's standards run, a right answer, fails grounding under both labels:

> **Summary:** Attachments must not be sent, and personal data must not be sent. For other request fields, send only those the AI use case needs; the standards do not identify specific RequestHub fields, so those must be selected based on the use case. A logging-standard conflict affects prompts or completions containing user-entered free text.
>
> **Recommendation:** Do not include attachments or personal data in model inputs. Claims without *fields*, such as `only those use case need*` or `minimum necessar* set`, would accept them, but they would also state E04's conclusion for *Attachments: send only those the use case needs* and *Send the minimum necessary set of attachments*, wrong answers that both labels fail. So neither was added, as `set of necessar*` wasn't for the 14:37 answer.
