# STD-AI-002 · Traceability and evaluation for AI features (v1.0)

Every AI call records the provider response or trace identifier and correlates it with the business record. Before release, an AI feature has a versioned evaluation dataset with at least ten labelled cases, including adversarial inputs, and the results are attached to the release.
